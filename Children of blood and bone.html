STORY OVERVIEW

Book Summary

In the kingdom of Orïsha, magic has been brutally suppressed, and the maji have been stripped of the power that once connected them to the elements. Zélie Adebola remembers a time when her people could summon fire, water, lightning, and other forces of nature, but after a ruthless king destroys magic and hunts those who once wielded it, that world seems lost forever. When Zélie discovers a chance to bring magic back, she is pulled into a dangerous journey that could change the fate of Orïsha. Along the way, she must confront powerful enemies, ancient secrets, and the growing threat of a kingdom determined to keep magic buried. With the help of unlikely allies, Zélie begins to understand that restoring magic will demand far more than courage—it will require her to face the pain of her past, challenge the forces that destroyed her people, and decide how much she is willing to sacrifice for their future.

A World Without Magic

Zélie has grown up under a ruthless regime that fears and oppresses her people, forcing the maji to live without the magic that once defined their culture. She has witnessed the pain caused by the destruction of magic and knows that restoring it could mean giving her people a chance to reclaim their identity and freedom. When an unexpected opportunity to bring magic back to Orïsha appears, Zélie is drawn into a dangerous journey filled with powerful enemies, hidden secrets, and difficult choices. As the stakes grow higher, she must learn to control her abilities, protect those she loves, and confront a kingdom determined to keep magic buried—while discovering that the fate of Orïsha may ultimately depend on her.

Destiny, Power, and Rebellion

Alongside her loyal brother Tzain and the mysterious princess Amari, Zélie is forced into a dangerous race against those determined to prevent magic from returning to Orïsha. As they travel through hostile lands, they face powerful enemies, deadly attacks, and choices that test the strength of their loyalty and their beliefs. Meanwhile, the conflict between the ruling class and the oppressed maji grows more intense, turning their journey into a struggle for survival, freedom, and control over the future of the kingdom. With every step, Zélie realizes that restoring magic may come at a devastating cost—and that the people she trusts most may be the ones who change the course of her destiny.

Before You Read

Questions That Shape the Story

Magic was violently suppressed by the ruling class, leaving the maji without the power that once defined their culture. The loss shapes Zélie's life and becomes central to her determination to restore it.

Zélie's connection to the maji and her ability to interact with ancient magical forces place her at the center of a dangerous mission. What begins as an impossible task becomes a fight for the future of her entire people.

Amari comes from the royal family responsible for the oppression of the maji, making trust extremely difficult. Yet her own experiences force her to question the world she was raised to protect.

Restoring magic could give the oppressed maji their power back, but it could also completely change the balance of power in Orïsha. Zélie must confront the consequences of what she is trying to unleash.
