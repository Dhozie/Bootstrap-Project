Tell Me Your Dream

STORY OVERVIEW

Book Summary

In the heart of modern America, three women with vastly different lives become linked by a series of brutal murders that leave investigators searching for a killer hiding in plain sight. As shocking secrets unravel and the line between reality and illusion begins to blur, each discovery reveals a darker truth, forcing everyone involved to question identity, trust, and the fragile nature of the human mind.

A Life Turned Upside Down

Ashley Patterson is a quiet and hardworking woman whose ordinary life is shattered when she becomes the prime suspect in a string of horrifying murders. As detectives dig deeper into the investigation, they discover that nothing about the case is as straightforward as it seems. Strange clues, conflicting evidence, and disturbing patterns begin to blur the line between truth and deception.

Beneath the Surface

What unfolds is a gripping psychological thriller that explores the hidden depths of the human mind, where fear, trauma, and identity collide. As shocking secrets emerge and long-buried memories surface, every revelation draws the investigation closer to a truth more unsettling than anyone could have imagined—one that challenges everything they thought they knew.

Before You Read

Questions That Haunt the Story

Ashley's ordinary life is shattered when she becomes the prime suspect in a series of horrifying murders. As the investigation unfolds, disturbing evidence begins pointing directly toward her.

Strange clues and conflicting evidence suggest that the murders may be connected to secrets buried deep within Ashley's past. The deeper detectives investigate, the more complicated the case becomes.

As buried memories and disturbing revelations begin to surface, the investigation takes an unexpected turn. Discovering the truth means looking beyond what appears to be an ordinary woman.

The case becomes a disturbing puzzle of identity, memory, and deception. Every new discovery raises another question, keeping the true culprit hidden until the mystery begins to unravel.
