STORY OVERVIEW

Book Summary

When twelve-year-old Regan MacNeil begins displaying terrifying and inexplicable changes in her behavior, her mother desperately searches for a medical explanation. But as the disturbing events become increasingly violent and supernatural, the family is forced to confront a possibility far more horrifying than illness: Regan may be possessed by a demonic force.

Something Is Inside Her

Chris MacNeil is a successful actress and devoted mother whose life begins to collapse when Regan starts experiencing strange physical and psychological disturbances. Doctors and specialists struggle to explain what is happening, but the increasingly terrifying behavior suggests that something far darker may be responsible.

The Battle Against Evil

As the supernatural presence grows stronger, Father Damien Karras, a priest struggling with his own crisis of faith, becomes involved in the case. Together with an experienced exorcist, he must confront an ancient evil in a terrifying battle between faith, doubt, sacrifice, and darkness.

Before You Read

Questions That Haunt the Story

Regan begins experiencing terrifying physical and behavioral changes that doctors cannot explain. As the disturbances become increasingly violent, the possibility of something supernatural begins to emerge.

Medical specialists investigate Regan's disturbing symptoms, but every explanation seems to fall short. The more they search for a rational answer, the more mysterious and frightening her condition becomes.

Father Karras is already struggling with doubt and personal grief when he becomes involved in Regan's case. Facing the supernatural forces surrounding her forces him to confront his deepest questions about faith, God, and evil.

As the battle against the demonic presence reaches its terrifying climax, the characters must rely on courage, faith, sacrifice, and the strength to face an evil they barely understand.
