STORY OVERVIEW

Book Summary

Lowen Ashleigh is a struggling writer who receives the opportunity of a lifetime when she is hired to complete the remaining books in a successful author's series. But when she arrives at the Crawford family's isolated home, she discovers a disturbing manuscript containing secrets that could destroy the family forever.

A House Full of Secrets

Lowen Ashleigh arrives at the Crawford home expecting to help complete Verity Crawford's unfinished novels. Instead, she finds herself drawn into the strange and unsettling world surrounding Verity, her husband Jeremy, and their family. What begins as a writing opportunity quickly becomes something far more dangerous.

The Manuscript

While searching through Verity's office, Lowen discovers an unfinished autobiography containing horrifying revelations about the family's past. The manuscript forces her to question everything she has been told and leaves her wondering whether Verity is truly helpless—or hiding something much darker.

Before You Read

Questions That Haunt the Story

Lowen discovers an unfinished manuscript hidden among Verity's papers. Its contents reveal disturbing secrets about the Crawford family and force Lowen to question everything she thought she knew.

Jeremy appears caring and vulnerable, but the discoveries Lowen makes gradually complicate her understanding of him. As more secrets emerge, she begins to wonder whether anyone in the house can truly be trusted.

Verity's condition leaves Lowen with more questions than answers. As Lowen becomes increasingly suspicious, she begins to wonder whether there is more to Verity's silence than anyone realizes.

As Lowen uncovers more disturbing information, the line between truth and deception becomes increasingly difficult to recognize. The deeper she goes, the more she must question the stories surrounding the Crawford family.
