STORY OVERVIEW

Book Summary

In the shadows of Depression-era Denver, A runaway boy with an extraordinary prophetic gift becomes the target of a ruthless Mafia family cursed by generations of demonic possession. As darkness spreads through both the streets and the unseen spiritual realm, Luke must discover whether faith alone is enough to break an ancient curse before countless lives—and souls—are lost.

A Gift With a Price

Luke, a ten-year-old boy escaping an abusive home during the Great Depression, finds refuge with a compassionate pastor and his wife in Denver. His new beginning takes an extraordinary turn when he discovers that he possesses a rare prophetic gift from God—a gift capable of revealing hidden truths, exposing evil, and changing lives. What first appears to be a blessing soon becomes a burden as powerful enemies begin hunting the young boy whose faith threatens everything they have built.

The Curse of the Fratelli Family

At the center of Denver's criminal underworld stands the Fratelli family, a ruthless Mafia dynasty haunted by an ancient curse stretching back centuries. Bound to demonic forces that promise power at the cost of countless souls, the family becomes entangled in a battle far greater than crime or revenge. As supernatural darkness spreads across the city, Luke must rely on unwavering faith, divine guidance, and extraordinary courage to uncover the truth and confront the evil that has plagued generations before it's too late.

Questions That Haunt the Story

Every answer uncovers another mystery. Explore a few of the secrets hidden inside Curse-breaker—without revealing the ending.

Luke possesses an extraordinary prophetic gift that places him at the center of a terrifying spiritual battle. But his ability may be far more dangerous than anyone realizes.

For generations, an influential Mafia family has carried a horrifying supernatural burden—one that feeds on fear, violence, and broken souls.

As darkness closes in, Luke discovers that courage alone isn't enough. Every decision forces him to choose between fear and faith—and the cost of either choice is enormous.

Sometimes the greatest horrors wear human faces. As Luke uncovers the truth, he realizes evil exists in more forms than he ever imagined.
