STORY OVERVIEW

Book Summary

Years after a devastating fire destroys her Gypsy Kumpania, Janeal Mikkado believes she has escaped the tragedy that changed her life. But the past she thought had been buried begins rising from the ashes when the people and secrets she believed were lost forever return.

The Fire That Changed Everything

Janeal has always dreamed of escaping her father's Gypsy culture and building a different life. When the powerful Salazar Sanso offers her a way out in exchange for helping recover a large amount of money, Janeal makes a choice that will haunt her long after the flames consume her community.

The Past Rises From the Ashes

Years later, Janeal's carefully constructed new identity begins to collapse when people she believed were dead suddenly reappear. As secrets surrounding the fire and the missing money begin to surface, Janeal is forced to confront the consequences of the choices she made—and the darkness still hiding within her heart.

Before You Read

Questions That Haunt the Story

Janeal desperately wants to escape the life she feels trapped in. When she discovers a large sum of money, she is forced to make a decision that will change the course of her life forever.

Powerful enemies connected to Salazar Sanso attack Janeal's Kumpania and leave destruction in their wake. But the full truth surrounding the fire and the people involved is more complicated than it first appears.

Janeal believes the fire has erased everyone and everything from her former life. Years later, however, people she believed were gone begin appearing again, forcing her to question everything she remembers about that night.

Janeal has spent years creating a new life and hiding from the choices she made. But when the past returns, she must finally confront what happened and decide whether she will continue running or face the consequences.
