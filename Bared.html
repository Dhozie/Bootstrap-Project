STORY OVERVIEW

Book Summary

Eva Tramell is determined to build a new life in New York, focused on her career and independence. Everything changes when she meets Gideon Cross, a powerful and intensely complicated man whose presence immediately disrupts the carefully controlled world she has created.

A Dangerous Attraction

Eva's new life takes an unexpected turn when she encounters Gideon Cross, a wealthy and influential businessman who is as magnetic as he is mysterious. Their attraction is immediate, but beneath the intensity lies a complicated history neither is prepared to reveal.

Beneath the Perfect Image

As Eva and Gideon grow closer, they discover that both are carrying painful experiences from their past. Their relationship becomes a struggle between trust, vulnerability, control, and the desire to overcome the wounds that continue to shape their lives.

Before You Read

Questions That Shape the Story

Eva is immediately captivated by Gideon's confidence, intensity, and mysterious personality. Their powerful attraction forces her to confront emotions she has tried to keep under control.

Both characters carry painful experiences from their pasts. As their relationship develops, those hidden wounds begin to influence the choices they make and the way they understand each other.

Their relationship is complicated by fear, vulnerability, and painful memories. Learning to trust each other becomes one of the greatest challenges they must face.

As their connection becomes deeper, both Eva and Gideon must decide whether they can confront their past experiences instead of allowing them to define their future.
